Service and protection boundaries

PicInside provides photo and video storage, photo notes, collections, favorites, optional Secure Album protection and backups. It also finds files with identical content across external folders you select, so you can review and remove duplicate copies.

External folder scanning is not a feature for importing every document format into Gallery. Regular Main/Sub original-file storage and Secure Album encryption are different protections. The service does not promise complete concealment, support for every format or recovery in every situation. Only process content you have the right to use.

Subscriptions and existing content

PicInside Pro is an App Store monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription. Check Apple's purchase screen for the actual price, period and renewal terms. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Manage or cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PicInside. If purchased access is missing, use Restore Purchases on the app's subscription screen. Follow Apple's refund-request guidance; outcomes depend on its procedure and applicable law. This does not remove our legal obligations or your consumer rights.

Expiration does not remove existing-content access, playback, search, organization, export, deletion, recovery, manual backup or restore. Basic's 10-item limit covers new external photo/video imports. Pro-only task execution and existing data rights are separate.

Pro-only features are creating collections and collection groups, detailed storage management and regenerable-cache cleanup, exact-duplicate finding inside InSpace, and running iCloud Automatic Backup. External-folder duplicate scanning is separate from the InSpace-only tool.

Registering and managing folders, running duplicate scans and rescans, and viewing aggregate duplicate-copy counts and sizes are free. The existing PicInside Pro subscription includes group-by-group details, individual filenames and locations, image/PDF/video previews, keeper recommendations, selection changes and cleanup of selected files. There is no separate product or price and no free-scan count limit. After a verified purchase or restoration, a valid existing result can open without another scan. Purchasing never deletes files; cleanup requires separate confirmation. The most recently Pro-opened detailed result and latest cleanup outcomes remain available read-only after expiration, without unlocking details for a new result or authorizing new cleanup. Cancelling scans, disconnecting folders, clearing scan history and contacting support remain free. Clearing history does not delete registered folders or source files. Import duplicate warnings are included, and Basic's 10-item import limit does not apply to external scan files.

Originals, deletion and backups

Importing does not automatically delete input originals from Photos or Files. External duplicate cleanup deletes selected files at their actual source location and may be irreversible depending on the provider. A batch can partially succeed, and cancellation does not undo completed deletions. Check the files you intend to keep and a separate backup before deleting. Local deletion, app deletion and subscription cancellation do not automatically remove external backups.

Passwords and recovery

A Recovery Key is not a copy of deleted media. Security-password reset is limited to Secure Folders previously prepared for that recovery method. An emergency backup needs the key associated with its creation; new keys do not automatically update old backups. Keep recovery means safely and separately. Main and Sub use separate authentication routes and keys. Preparing administrator access preserves the existing Sub password login and content; it does not replace a Secure Folder’s separate security password.
