Operating brand and scope

PicInside's legal business name is 스튜디오 JS (Studio JS), and its public brand is Studio JS. Its representative directly operates this one-person business. This policy distinguishes app processing, external services you choose, support inquiries and public web pages.

Media and on-device protection

Regular Main/Sub InSpaces keep the original files received during import without recompressing or re-encoding them. Supported formats and types of originals may depend on iOS and the file provider.

PicInside does not add its own encryption to these media files; iOS file protection and the app lock protect access. Preserving originals can also retain embedded capture or location information.

Library details such as names, types, sizes, dates, dimensions, video duration and favorites, photo notes and tags, and derived thumbnails are processed on-device and protected by authenticated encryption in their respective stores. The current app has no feature that sends these to a PicInside-operated server and no analytics or advertising SDK.

Secure Folder and recovery

Secure Folder (Secure Album) adds authenticated encryption with a separate key for each media file, without recompressing or re-encoding the photo or video. A fully decrypted export matches the input original byte for byte.

A Recovery Key can reset a forgotten security password only for a Secure Folder previously prepared for recovery with that key. If both the password and valid recovery means are lost, locked content cannot be recovered. A Recovery Key is not a backup of deleted files.

Main, Sub and existing content

Main and Sub use separate authentication routes and keys. An existing Sub can be linked for management by verifying its credential once while authenticated as the Main administrator; a new Sub is linked during setup. The linked Sub card on administrator Home then opens Sub without another credential prompt.

The link adds an encrypted way to unlock the Sub key; it does not combine the spaces' keys or re-encrypt regular media files.

Signing in to Sub does not grant administrator authority. Cancelling link preparation or having no prepared link preserves the original Sub login and media; a restore or key change may require preparing the link again.

Legacy Sub Secure Data is managed through Main administrator settings → Secure Folder → Legacy Sub Secure Data. Access to existing content, including viewing, export, backup and restore, does not require a new purchase. A Secure Folder protected by a security password cannot be opened through administrator sign-in alone; its security password or a valid recovery method prepared in advance is required.

External folder scans and cache

External duplicate scans cover the folders you select and authorize. File information and comparison data stay on your device for rescans.

The stored data includes access bookmarks, folder names and location labels, relative paths and filenames, sizes, dates, identity information, first-seen order and content fingerprints. Fingerprints are comparison data, not copies of the original files.

This separate scan store uses authenticated encryption with an independent file key, iOS file protection and exclusion from OS backup. It is not protected by the Secure Folder security password. It is not cleared whenever the app closes or after a fixed interval.

The same encrypted store also holds the full-content SHA-256 used in the scan, the latest scan result, the last detailed result opened with Pro, and the latest cleanup record. It stores changes to your keeper and cleanup selections so they can be restored when you reopen the same result. Previous choices are not applied unchanged to a different result or file membership.

In administrator Home → Find & Clean Up Duplicate Files → Find Duplicate Files, choose the − button (Remove from Scan List) for that folder to remove the registration and related scan information, not the source files. This does not revoke every OS/provider permission or clear all system caches.

Clear Scan History removes the scan cache, saved results, latest cleanup record and saved selections, while keeping registered folders and actual files. Disconnecting a folder invalidates its registration and cache, the latest result and saved selections; it also removes previously viewed results and cleanup records that include that folder.

Preview, deletion and file providers

Duplicates are confirmed using full-content fingerprints, not filenames or samples alone. Deletion requires your explicit selection and confirmation and revalidation of both the copy to keep and the copy to remove.

Saving keeper and cleanup selections does not save deletion approval. After a background or authentication boundary, cleanup confirmation is required again. Restoring selections or making files ready never starts deletion automatically.

External deletion does not move files to PicInside's internal Trash. It may be permanent depending on the provider, with no guaranteed recovery period. Cancellation does not undo completed deletions.

Previews may use system Quick Look and your selected file provider. Online files may need downloading. Providers may process requested files, paths and connection information; their policies apply to network processing and system caches. PicInside cannot guarantee immediate removal of every system cache.

Thumbnails PicInside creates for external result lists are cached only in memory, with limits on item count and memory use, not as separate disk copies. System and file-provider caches are separate.

Files, iCloud Drive and CloudKit backups

Manual emergency backups are separately encrypted and saved to a location you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Restore may require the Recovery Key used at creation; a new key does not automatically update old backups.

Enabling iCloud Automatic Backup in Pro stores encrypted media chunks, detailed catalog data and recovery material in your private CloudKit database. Object identifiers, chunk order and size, backup time, item count, total size and device identifiers are operational data, not all PicInside ciphertext.

After Main administrator authentication, you can turn automatic backup off regardless of subscription status. Turning it back on still requires verified Pro access and recovery setup. Once Off is saved, reopening the app or resubscribing does not turn it on automatically.

Local deletion, turning off automatic backup or subscription expiration does not delete external backups. Some old snapshot metadata is removed, but unused encrypted chunks may remain for recovery safety.

Manage a manual backup by selecting that file at its saved location in Files. Shared or copied backups remain separately. The app has no control for deleting all private CloudKit backups and residual chunks. Do not delete individual chunks needed for recovery. We cannot delete your private backup on your behalf through support email. Apple's and your chosen storage service's retention and management conditions also apply.

Apple billing and data rights

Apple handles purchases and payments. The app checks verified StoreKit transaction, renewal and entitlement information. Its protected local entitlement record stores the record-format version and state category, verification time and validity deadline, not payment-card or Apple account details.

After a subscription expires, access to existing content stored in your PicInside library, playback, search, organization, export, deletion, restore and manual backup remain available. Basic's 10-item limit concerns new photos and videos imported from outside the app; it does not trigger automatic deletion of existing content.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Deleting an item from Gallery moves it to that space's Trash. You can restore it within 14 days of the move or permanently delete it sooner. Expired items are purged when that space is read again; deletion at the exact deadline in the background is not guaranteed. Secure Album Trash is separate and requires valid access to that album for restore, permanent deletion or expiry cleanup.

Input originals in Photos or Files are not automatically deleted. Deleting the app can remove local content without removing every Keychain credential or external backup. The app has no user control to delete all PicInside Keychain items. Turning off Face ID removes its biometric key copy, not all login or recovery information.

General inquiry information is deleted within 90 days after handling is complete. This is a maximum; information no longer needed is deleted without delay, even before then. Only records that applicable law requires are retained separately for the legally required period and deleted after it ends. General usage inquiries and consumer complaint or dispute records are classified separately.

For PicInside Pro records handled by us, Article 6 of Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce and Article 6 of its Enforcement Decree require five years for contract or withdrawal records and payment or service-supply records, and three years for consumer complaint or dispute-handling records. We limit retention to information actually handled and needed for that transaction or case, such as identifiers and contact details, product, date and amount, and the request and outcome. These periods do not apply to all general inquiries or unrelated attachments. Required records are separated from general inquiries, used only for statutory retention purposes, and deleted when the period ends.

The representative records completion dates, checks them regularly and confirms timely deletion of due messages and managed copies, including Gmail Trash. Management records do not unnecessarily duplicate message bodies or attachments or keep them indefinitely. This does not mean automatic Gmail deletion is configured.
